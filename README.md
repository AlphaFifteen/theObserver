# theObserver
You control a quirky, fast-moving circle. The goal is simple: click on it before it gets away Core Loop: A circle with a "pupil" bounces around the canvas. Your task is to click on it. Each successful click increases your score and teleports the circle to a new, random location. Interaction and Feedback: Clicking the circle gives you a point and resets its position. If you miss, the circle will "push" away from your click, making it a bit harder to catch. A cooldown prevents rapid-fire clicking. Visual and Audio Style: The game has a hand-drawn, retro feel with a dotted grid, gradient circles, and playful "eye" emojis. The score is displayed, and the buttons provide clear actions for playing and resetting.
